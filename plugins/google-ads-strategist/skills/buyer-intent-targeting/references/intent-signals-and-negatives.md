# Buyer-Intent Signal Lists & Default Negative Keywords

Detailed per-class query/SERP signals, ad copy framing cues, and Day-1 phrase-match negative keyword lists for `buyer-intent-targeting`.

## Detailed Intent Signals

### 1. Informational (NEVER bid)
- **Query signals**: "how to", "how do I", "what is", "why does", "when should", "guide", "tutorial", "explained", "meaning", "definition", "free", "template", "example", "sample", "DIY", "manual", "step by step", long-tail question forms ("can I file my own annual report").
- **SERP signals**: "People Also Ask" dominates top fold; organic results are listicles, blogs, Wikipedia; few/no shopping or paid ads above fold; long-form featured snippets.

### 2. Navigational (Own brand defensively only)
- **Query signals**: specific brand name (`<brand>`), brand + generic modifier (`<brand> login`, `<brand> price`), specific product name + company.
- **Action**: bid on own brand as a defensive moat (highest CVR); never bid on competitor brands without explicit conquesting strategy and legal clearance.

### 3. Commercial Investigation (PRIORITY target)
- **Query signals**: "best [category]", "[category] for [audience]", "[brand A] vs [brand B]", "[brand] review", "[brand] alternative", "[brand] pricing", "cheap/affordable [category]", "[category] cost", "top [category]".
- **SERP signals**: multiple paid ads above fold; comparison articles mixed with commercial pages; shopping/service listings; "People Also Ask" present but not dominant.
- **Copy framing**: lead with differentiation ("Unlike X, we Y"), show price/range if advantageous, add pricing/comparison sitelinks, emphasize proof (reviews, customer count, certifications).

### 4. Transactional (HIGHEST priority target)
- **Query signals**: "buy", "order", "hire", "book", "get", "purchase", "[service] near me", "[service] in [city]", "[category] price/cost", "[category] online", "[service] today", "sign up for / register [category]".
- **SERP signals**: paid ads top AND bottom; shopping carousel or service listings; local pack for local services; very few informational results above fold.
- **Copy framing**: lead with action ("Start Now", "Order in 5 Minutes", "Book a Slot Today"), honest deadline urgency, price up front if competitive, CTA button phrasing, full extensions (sitelinks, callouts, price, promotion, structured snippets).
